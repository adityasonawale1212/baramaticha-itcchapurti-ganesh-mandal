A live, production-grade web portal built for **Ichhapurti Bhakt Mandal (बारामती)** to showcase their annual Ganesh Chaturthi festival, daily Aarti schedules, event timelines, and a rich interactive Visarjan photo/video gallery.

## 🌐 Live Website
👉 **[Visit Live Portal](https://baramaticha-itchapurti-ganesh-mandal.pages.dev)**

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📱 **Mobile-First & Fully Responsive:** Engineered to look flawless across all screen sizes (desktop, tablet, iPhone, Android) with custom hamburger navigation.
- ⏱️ **Real-time Countdown Timer:** JavaScript countdown ticking dynamically to the arrival of Ganesh Chaturthi.
- 📸 **Dynamic Media Gallery & Filter System:**
  - Categorized tabs: `All`, `Photos`, and `Videos`.
  - Custom full-screen **Lightbox Popup** supporting both high-resolution images and native HTML5 video streaming with full audio controls.
  - Video cards equipped with interactive play button badges and smooth zoom animations.
- 🕉️ **Bilingual & Cultural Typography:** Designed with clean Google Devanagari web fonts (`Noto Sans Devanagari`, `Baloo 2`) with festival color palettes (Saffron, Deep Maroon, Gold).
- 📅 **Schedule & Events Timeline:** Structured daily Aarti timings and festival event schedules.
- 📍 **Social & Contact Integration:** Direct one-click Instagram profile redirection and telephone contact links.
